# Changelog

User-facing notes. Newest first.

## 2026-09-22: A new look

**Redesigned from the ground up.** Arena OS now looks and feels like a broadcast control room.

- **A liquid-metal Arena mark.** It resolves in the intro, sits in the sidebar (hover it), and takes on the accent of the game you are in.
- **A short intro** the first time you open the app each session: under 3 seconds, skip it any time with **Esc**. It never plays if you prefer reduced motion.
- **Game containers feel different.** Switching between games now sweeps in that game's colour, glyph and name, and each game has its own background art.
- **Real glass** on the sign-in and onboarding screens: the card genuinely bends the light behind it. On devices that can't do this it falls back to a frosted look.
- **Bolder, calmer pages.** Big condensed titles, small mono labels, and fewer boxes: sections are separated by space and hairlines instead of cards.
- **Real numbers, drawn well.** The overview shows your containers, members, pending invitations and a 14-day activity line. Team pages show role coverage. If there's nothing to show, it says so. Nothing is made up.
- **Command palette 2.0.** Press **⌘K** (or **Ctrl+K**, or **?**) for actions like "Invite someone", your recent items, and quick switching. Try **g** then **m** / **a** / **s** / **o** to jump around without the mouse.
- **Sound, if you want it.** Subtle cues for confirmations and switching containers. Off by default; toggle it in the top bar or Settings.
- **New illustrations** for empty screens, and clearer wording throughout.

## 2026-09-21: Foundation

Sign in, organisations, game containers, invitations, roles and permissions, audit log, guardian consent for under-18 players, light and dark themes, and an in-app motion setting.
